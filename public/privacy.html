Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

DAKboard Calendar Widgets ("the app") is a private, single-household dashboard that displays a Google Calendar schedule and local weather on a wall-mounted display. This policy describes how it handles data.

What the app accesses

With the account owner's authorization, the app uses read-only access to Google Calendar (the calendar.readonly scope) to list calendars and read events. It never creates, modifies, or deletes calendar data.

How data is used

  • Calendar events are fetched on demand to render the schedule display.
  • Requests to Google are made from the app's own server (a Cloudflare Worker); Google credentials are stored only as server-side secrets and are never sent to the browser.
  • The app does not sell, share, or transmit calendar data to any third party.

Data retention

The app does not maintain its own database. Calendar and weather data are fetched transiently to render the display and are not persisted beyond the lifetime of a request.

Third-party services

The app calls the Google Calendar API, Open-Meteo, the U.S. National Weather Service, and CheckWX to retrieve schedule and weather information. Their handling of data is governed by their own privacy policies.

Revoking access

The account owner can revoke the app's access at any time at Google Account permissions.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to the account owner who operates this dashboard.
